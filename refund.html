Refund Policy

How refunds work for Node AI subscriptions and token top-ups, depending on where you bought them.

01Overview

We want you to be happy with Node AI. Node AI is sold through the App Store and Google Play, so refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules.

02Purchases in the App Store

Subscriptions and top-ups bought on iPhone or iPad are billed by Apple. We cannot issue refunds for them ourselves. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple decides on each request.

03Purchases in Google Play

Purchases made on Android are billed by Google. You can request a refund in the Google Play app or at play.google.com under Payments & subscriptions. Google’s refund rules apply.

04Free trials

If your plan starts with a free trial, you will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. Once the trial converts to a paid plan, the rules above apply.

05Token top-ups

Top-up tokens are valid for 12 months. Refund requests for top-ups go through Apple or Google, like any other in-app purchase. Tokens already used for generations generally cannot be refunded.

06Cancelling a subscription

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Node AI.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the paid period. Top-up tokens stay on your balance.

07How to request a refund

Refunds are requested from Apple or Google using the links above. If something went wrong with a generation or your tokens, email support@node-ai.app with the email on your account and a short description. We reply within 3 business days and can restore tokens where appropriate.

08Contact us

Billing questions: support@node-ai.app.

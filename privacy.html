01Who we are

Company legal name, registered at Registered address, is responsible for your personal data when you use Node AI ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, contact privacy@node-ai.app.

02Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email and sign-in details (for example Sign in with Apple or Google).
  • Content — prompts, messages, files and images you send, and the results generated for you.
  • Purchase data — plan, token balance and transaction history. Card details are handled by Apple, Google or our payment provider Payment provider; we never see full card numbers.
  • Usage and device data — app version, device type, language, IP address, crash reports and how features are used.

03How we use your data

  • to provide the service: run your requests, store your history and sync it across devices;
  • to process payments and manage your plan and tokens;
  • to keep Node AI safe: prevent abuse, fraud and violations of our Terms;
  • to fix bugs and improve features;
  • to send service messages such as "your video is ready" and, if you agree, product news.

We do not use your content to train our own AI models.

04AI model providers

To answer your requests we send your prompts and files to the AI model you choose, or the one selected by Auto mode. These providers include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Midjourney, Kling, Suno. They process the data to generate a response under their own terms and data policies.

05When we share data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • service providers that help us run Node AI (hosting, analytics, payments, customer support), under contracts that protect your data;
  • AI model providers, as described above;
  • authorities, when required by law or to protect rights and safety;
  • a buyer or successor, if Node AI is involved in a merger or acquisition.

06Cookies and analytics

Our website and web app use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences and understand how the product is used. We use Analytics tools. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

07How long we keep data

We keep account data while your account is active. Chat history and generations are kept until you delete them or your account. After account deletion we remove or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer by law (for example, payment records).

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict some processing. You can delete conversations and your account in Profile settings, or email privacy@node-ai.app. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

09Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and regular reviews to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep your account details private.

10Children

Node AI is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. When we transfer data internationally, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new date and notify you of significant changes in the app or by email.

13Contact us

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@node-ai.app.
